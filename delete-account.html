ACCOUNT AND DATA DELETION

Delete your RACIA account

RACIA users can request permanent account deletion inside the app or by email.

Delete inside the app

  1. Open RACIA and sign in.
  2. Open Profile.
  3. Select Account.
  4. Select Delete account.
  5. Enter your password and confirm deletion.

Request deletion without the app

Email mr.qachibaia44@gmail.com from your recovery email address with the subject RACIA account deletion. Include your callsign, but do not send your password. We may ask for additional information to verify account ownership before completing the request.

Data that is deleted

Deletion removes the account, authentication sessions, profile, memberships, blocks, registered devices, push tokens, preferences and associated SOS/location records.

Limited security records may be retained only when required by law or necessary to prevent fraud and abuse.

Privacy Policy

RACIA is operated by Nika Kachibaia. This policy explains how RACIA processes personal information when you use the mobile application and related backend services.

Information we process

  • Account information: your name, unique callsign, recovery email address, password hash and authentication session information.
  • Group and app activity: groups and channels you create or join, memberships, blocked users, preferences, invite activity and push-notification registration token.
  • Location: precise location only when you grant permission and use nearby discovery or choose to include your position with an SOS. SOS coordinates and status are stored so the alert can be delivered, retried, cancelled or resolved.
  • Voice communications: microphone audio is transmitted in real time to people on your active channel using encrypted WebRTC connections. RACIA does not record or store the content of voice conversations.
  • Camera and QR codes: the camera is used to scan group invitation QR codes. Images are processed on the device and are not uploaded by RACIA.
  • Bluetooth and device information: permission state, compatible accessory identity, pairing information and battery/PTT status when you connect a Racia Link accessory.
  • Technical information: limited connection, security and error information needed to operate, protect and troubleshoot the service.

Why we process information

We use this information to create and secure accounts, provide push-to-talk and group features, deliver notifications and SOS alerts, show nearby channels when requested, connect compatible accessories, prevent abuse and maintain the service. RACIA does not sell personal information and does not use it for third-party advertising.

Service providers and transmissions

RACIA relies on service providers for hosting, PostgreSQL data storage, email delivery, push notifications, map tiles and internet connectivity such as STUN/TURN relaying. These providers process only the information needed to perform their services. Voice traffic may pass through a TURN relay when devices cannot connect directly, but remains encrypted in transit and is not retained by RACIA.

Retention and deletion

Account information is retained while your account is active. Operational records are kept only as long as needed to provide and secure the service. You can permanently delete your account inside RACIA from Profile → Account → Delete account. After password confirmation, RACIA deletes the account, authentication sessions, memberships, blocks, registered devices, push tokens, preferences and associated SOS/location records.

You may also request deletion through the public account-deletion instructions or by contacting us at mr.qachibaia44@gmail.com.

Your choices

You can deny or revoke microphone, location, camera, notification and Bluetooth permissions in your device settings. Features that depend on a denied permission will not be available. Location sharing in SOS can also be controlled in RACIA settings.

Security

RACIA uses transport encryption, hashed passwords, short-lived access tokens, rotating refresh sessions and access controls intended to protect your information. No system can guarantee absolute security.

Children

RACIA is not directed to children under 18. The intended Google Play audience is adults.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy as RACIA changes. The current version and its effective date will be published at this URL. Questions or privacy requests can be sent to mr.qachibaia44@gmail.com.
